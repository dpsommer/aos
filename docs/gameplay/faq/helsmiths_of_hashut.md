# Helsmiths of Hashut FAQ

Official rulings that concern Helsmiths of Hashut, from the [Rules Updates, September 2026](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_23-09_aos_core&key_rule_updates-eu6fu82jl3-dlyuiignte.pdf). They're paraphrased; the PDF (page numbers below) has the exact wording.

## Battletome (p. 50)

**Q:** When 'Harness Daemonic Power' removes daemonic power points from units, are they kept to reallocate or lost?

**A:** Lost.

**Q:** Can the Curse-Steel Battery's 'Display of Total Power' be used if one of its War Machines has been destroyed?

**A:** Yes.

**Q:** In a Taar's Grand Forgehost army, does a unit lose a daemonic power point it gained during the round (e.g. from the Infernal Cohort with Hashutite Spears' 'Conquered Lands') at the end of the battle round?

**A:** No.

## Other rulings

**Q:** If a unit is affected by two abilities that each ignore the first damage point in a phase (e.g. the Ashen Elder's 'Stoked Fanaticism' and Taar's Grand Forgehost's 'Reinforce Daemonsteel'), does it ignore the first two? And if it took damage before being affected, does it ignore the next damage point? (Core Rules 17.0, pp. 5–6)

**A:** No and no. It only ever ignores the first damage point dealt to it in that phase.
