# Sons of Behemat FAQ

Official rulings that concern Sons of Behemat, from the [Rules Updates, September 2026](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_23-09_aos_core&key_rule_updates-eu6fu82jl3-dlyuiignte.pdf). The faction pack section has no FAQs; this comes from another section. It's paraphrased; the PDF (page number below) has the exact wording.

## Other rulings

**Q:** If an ability gives a unit an enhancement during a battle (e.g. the 'More Stuff for Me Collection' ability) and the enhancement costs points, does the roster need spare points to pay for it? (Advanced Rules, Army Composition 4.1, p. 12)

**A:** No. Enhancements gained during a battle cost no points.
