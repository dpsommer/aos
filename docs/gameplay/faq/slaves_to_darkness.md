# Slaves to Darkness FAQ

Official rulings that concern Slaves to Darkness, from the [Rules Updates, October 2026](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_28-10_aos_core_rules_and_key_downloads_rules_updates-aghvsvstmk-u8wyh173fe.pdf). They're paraphrased; the PDF (page numbers below) has the exact wording.

## Battletome (p. 70)

**Q:** When 'Dark Apotheosis' sets up a Daemon Prince, must it be on the roster, and does it cost points?

**A:** No to both.

**Q:** If Archaon's 'Eye of Sheerian' forces the Slaves to Darkness player to take the first turn after taking the second turn in the previous round (a double turn), do they suffer the double-turn penalties, such as not picking a battle tactic or the opponent counting as the underdog?

**A:** No. The penalties only apply if you chose to go first.

**Q:** Can a unit use 'Pledge to Chaos' while it isn't on the battlefield, e.g. in reserve in a Silver Tower?

**A:** No.

**Q:** Can a Gaunt Summoner use 'Minions of the Silver Tower' more than once to set up several units in a Silver Tower?

**A:** No (Core Rules 5.3, the Rules of One).

**Q:** Can a Slaves to Darkness army include more than 1 Ensorcelled Banner?

**A:** No. They're enhancements, and an army can only take 1 enhancement from each enhancement table (Army Composition 4.1).

**Q:** Can Regiments of Renown be included in a Legion of the First Prince army?

**A:** Yes.

**Q:** Can a Daemon Prince from the Godmarked Ascendant Regiment of Renown be given enhancements?

**A:** Yes. Regiment of Renown units can use a faction's rules if they have a keyword matching the faction's name (Army Composition 3.5), and the Godmarked Ascendant adds such a keyword.

**Q:** With the General's Handbook 2026-27 battlepack, does the effect applied by 'For the Glory of the Dark Gods' change when the player's fury level changes, or is it fixed when the ability is used?

**A:** It changes with the fury level.

## Other rulings

**Q:** In 'Pick a friendly non-Hero Legion of the First Prince Daemon Infantry or Cavalry unit' (and similar wordings), does 'Cavalry unit' mean any Cavalry unit? (Core Rules 5.1, p. 4)

**A:** No. It means a friendly non-Hero Legion of the First Prince Daemon Cavalry unit.
