# Cities of Sigmar FAQ

Official rulings that concern Cities of Sigmar, from the [Rules Updates, September 2026](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_23-09_aos_core&key_rule_updates-eu6fu82jl3-dlyuiignte.pdf). They're paraphrased; the PDF (page numbers below) has the exact wording.

## Battletome (p. 39)

**Q:** Does the 'Overheating' ability remove one heat token, or one for each 5+?

**A:** One heat token for each 5+.

## Other rulings

**Q:** If a Hero can join another Hero's regiment (e.g. an Assassin joining a Dreadlord on Black Dragon), does it take the place of a non-Hero unit in that regiment? (Advanced Rules, Army Composition 3.1, p. 12)

**A:** Yes.
