# Cities of Sigmar FAQ

Official rulings that concern Cities of Sigmar, from the [Rules Updates, October 2026](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_28-10_aos_core_rules_and_key_downloads_rules_updates-aghvsvstmk-u8wyh173fe.pdf). They're paraphrased; the PDF (page numbers below) has the exact wording.

## Battletome (p. 39)

**Q:** Does the 'Overheating' ability remove one heat token, or one for each 5+?

**A:** One heat token for each 5+.
