# Maggotkin of Nurgle FAQ

Official rulings that concern Maggotkin of Nurgle, from the [Rules Updates, October 2026](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_28-10_aos_core_rules_and_key_downloads_rules_updates-aghvsvstmk-u8wyh173fe.pdf). They're paraphrased; the PDF (page numbers below) has the exact wording.

## Battletome (p. 58)

**Q:** If a unit within 3" of a plague progenitor picked as the target of the 'Spread' effect of 'Blessed by the Plaguefather' already has the Disease keyword, does the 'Plague Progenitor' effect still inflict D3 mortal damage on it?

**A:** Yes.

**Q:** If a plague progenitor would heal in the first battle round, can it remove disease instead?

**A:** No.

**Q:** Does 'Desperate Remedies' remove the Diseased keyword from an undamaged unit?

**A:** Yes.

**Q:** Can 0 Mire Kelpies be picked when using 'Contagious Catarrh'?

**A:** Yes.

**Q:** If Mire Kelpies were picked for 'Contagious Catarrh' and a 1 is rolled, are they removed from the unit?

**A:** No.

**Q:** If 'Wracked with Disease' destroys a diseased enemy unit and so triggers 'Burst Pustules', is 'Wracked with Disease' finished first?

**A:** Yes, as models are removed after an ability has been resolved.

**Q:** Can Diseased units in reserve (e.g. placed there by the Disciples of Tzeentch's 'Smoke and Mirrors') be picked as the target of 'Wracked with Disease'?

**A:** No.

## Other rulings

**Q:** What does 'a Save characteristic of 3+ or better' mean in abilities such as the 'Cloying Quagmire' spell? (Core Rules 5.2, p. 4)

**A:** Any Save characteristic better than 3+, such as 2+.

**Q:** If a Maggotkin of Nurgle unit is the target of Ushoran's 'Glimpse of Delusion' and a model in it is slain, does that trigger 'Vectors of Contagion'? (Flesh-eater Courts battletome, p. 44)

**A:** No.
