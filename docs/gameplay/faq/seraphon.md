# Seraphon FAQ

Official rulings that concern Seraphon, from the [Rules Updates, September 2026](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_23-09_aos_core&key_rule_updates-eu6fu82jl3-dlyuiignte.pdf). The faction pack section has no FAQs; these come from other sections. They're paraphrased; the PDF (page numbers below) has the exact wording.

## Other rulings

**Q:** Is the halved Move characteristic from the 'Lord of Space and Time' heroic trait permanent? (Other Digital Downloads, Scourge of Aqshy, p. 34)

**A:** No, it only applies in the opponent's next turn.

**Q:** If a rule picks units with one keyword or another (e.g. 'Pick up to 3 friendly Skink Infantry or Cavalry units'), can the picks mix keywords? (Core Rules 28.0, p. 7)

**A:** Yes, for example 1 Skink Infantry unit and 2 Skink Cavalry units.

**Q:** If the Realmshaper Engine's 'Power Unleashed' picks a faction terrain feature with a unit placed on it (e.g. a Shrine Luminor), can the unit on it also be picked as a target? (Advanced Rules, Terrain 1.5, p. 10)

**A:** Yes.
