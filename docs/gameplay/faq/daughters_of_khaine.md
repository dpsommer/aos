# Daughters of Khaine FAQ

Official rulings that concern Daughters of Khaine, from the [Rules Updates, September 2026](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_23-09_aos_core&key_rule_updates-eu6fu82jl3-dlyuiignte.pdf). The battletome section has no FAQs; these come from other sections. They're paraphrased; the PDF (page numbers below) has the exact wording.

## Other rulings

**Q:** If The Shadow Queen joins Morathi-Khaine's regiment, does she take the place of a non-Hero unit in it? (Advanced Rules, Army Composition 3.1, p. 12)

**A:** Yes.

**Q:** How does damage from the Creeping Corruption battleplan's 'Pulsing Life Energies' interact with The Shadow Queen's 'Iron Heart of Khaine'? (General's Handbook 2025-26, p. 24)

**A:** That damage is inflicted in the start of battle round, before either player's turn, so 'Iron Heart of Khaine' has no effect on it, and it doesn't count towards that ability's damage allocation limit in the next turn.
