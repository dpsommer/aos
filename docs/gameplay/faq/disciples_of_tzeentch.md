# Disciples of Tzeentch FAQ

Official rulings that concern Disciples of Tzeentch, from the [Rules Updates, September 2026](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_23-09_aos_core&key_rule_updates-eu6fu82jl3-dlyuiignte.pdf). They're paraphrased; the PDF (page numbers below) has the exact wording.

## Battletome (p. 42)

**Q:** If a Lord of Change's 'Temporal Manipulation' is measured from an Argent Shard because of 'Magical Focus', must the target end its move nearer the Lord of Change or the Argent Shard?

**A:** The Lord of Change. 'Magical Focus' only affects picking targets.

**Q:** What happens to a persisting effect on a unit that is removed from the battlefield and set up in reserve, e.g. a unit under 'Shield of Fate' that uses 'Smoke and Mirrors'?

**A:** The effect still applies for its duration. In the example, the unit keeps 'Shield of Fate' if it's set up on the battlefield again before its commanding player's next hero phase.

**Q:** If a Jade Obelisks unit is placed in reserve or destroyed, does 'Chosen by the Stone' keep affecting the enemy unit its Idolarc is next to?

**A:** Yes, it's a persisting effect.

## Other rulings

**Q:** Are non-passive abilities such as 'Burning Wyrdflame' optional? (Core Rules 5.0, p. 4)

**A:** Yes. Passive abilities and abilities that say they must be used are mandatory when possible; all others are optional.

**Q:** If a Regiment of Renown such as the Coven of Thryx includes manifestations, do they replace the army's manifestation lore? (Advanced Rules, Army Composition 3.5, p. 12)

**A:** No, they're in addition to it.
