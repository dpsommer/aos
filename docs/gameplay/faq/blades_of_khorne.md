# Blades of Khorne FAQ

Official rulings that concern Blades of Khorne, from the [Rules Updates, September 2026](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_23-09_aos_core&key_rule_updates-eu6fu82jl3-dlyuiignte.pdf). They're paraphrased; the PDF (page numbers below) has the exact wording.

## Battletome (p. 37)

**Q:** Does 'Blood-drenched' affect Skarbrand in The Exiled One Regiment of Renown?

**A:** No. It's a Blades of Khorne battle trait, so it has no effect on a unit in a Regiment of Renown.

**Q:** Does Skarbrand's 'Skarbrand's Rage' mean he's affected by 'Blood-drenched' in the first turn?

**A:** No.

## Other rulings

**Q:** Does destroying a faction terrain feature or a manifestation count for abilities that need a unit to be destroyed, such as 'The Blood Tithe'? (Advanced Rules, Terrain 1.5 and Magic 7.0, pp. 10–11)

**A:** No.
