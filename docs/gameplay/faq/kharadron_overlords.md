# Kharadron Overlords FAQ

Official rulings that concern Kharadron Overlords, from the [Rules Updates, October 2026](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_28-10_aos_core_rules_and_key_downloads_rules_updates-aghvsvstmk-u8wyh173fe.pdf). They're paraphrased; the PDF (page numbers below) has the exact wording.

## Battletome (p. 54)

**Q:** Can an Arkanaut Frigate use 'Assault Boat' after using a Run ability that turn, if Brokk Grungsson's 'Command the Fleet' picked it earlier in the turn?

**A:** Yes.

**Q:** Can 'Assault Boat' be combined with 'Counter-charge'?

**A:** Yes.

**Q:** If the 'Blazebeard and Sons Bamboozling Flakgun' removes a dice from an opponent's charge roll and they then use 'Forward to Victory', how many dice do they re-roll?

**A:** The dice left after the removal. A 2-dice charge roll with 1 dice removed is re-rolled with 1 dice.

## Other rulings

**Q:** If an ability gives a unit an enhancement during a battle (e.g. the 'Special Procurement' ability) and the enhancement costs points, does the roster need spare points to pay for it? (Advanced Rules, Army Composition 4.1, p. 12)

**A:** No. Enhancements gained during a battle cost no points.
