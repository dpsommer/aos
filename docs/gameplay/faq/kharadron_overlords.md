# Kharadron Overlords FAQ

Official rulings that concern Kharadron Overlords, from the [Rules Updates, September 2026](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_23-09_aos_core&key_rule_updates-eu6fu82jl3-dlyuiignte.pdf). They're paraphrased; the PDF (page numbers below) has the exact wording.

## Battletome (p. 54)

**Q:** Can an Arkanaut Frigate use 'Assault Boat' after using a Run ability that turn, if Brokk Grungsson's 'Command the Fleet' picked it earlier in the turn?

**A:** Yes.

**Q:** Can 'Assault Boat' be combined with 'Counter-charge'?

**A:** Yes.

**Q:** If the 'Blazebeard and Sons Bamboozling Flakgun' removes a dice from an opponent's charge roll and they then use 'Forward to Victory', how many dice do they re-roll?

**A:** The dice left after the removal. A 2-dice charge roll with 1 dice removed is re-rolled with 1 dice.
