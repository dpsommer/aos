# Hedonites of Slaanesh FAQ

Official rulings that concern Hedonites of Slaanesh, from the [Rules Updates, October 2026](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_28-10_aos_core_rules_and_key_downloads_rules_updates-aghvsvstmk-u8wyh173fe.pdf). The battletome section has no FAQs; these come from other sections. They're paraphrased; the PDF (page numbers below) has the exact wording.

## Other rulings

**Q:** When has a player 'finished setting up their army' for determining the active player? (Core Rules 12.0, p. 5)

**A:** When all units in their army have been deployed. Deployment Phase abilities that aren't Deploy abilities, such as The Masque's 'The Endless Dance', happen after the army has finished setting up.

**Q:** Can a Warmaster (e.g. Sigvald, Prince of Slaanesh) be included in another Hero's regiment? (Advanced Rules, Army Composition 3.1, p. 12)

**A:** Only if the army includes another Warmaster unit, since the general must lead a regiment and must be a Warmaster if the army has any.
