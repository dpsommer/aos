# Idoneth Deepkin FAQ

Official rulings that concern Idoneth Deepkin, from the [Rules Updates, September 2026](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_23-09_aos_core&key_rule_updates-eu6fu82jl3-dlyuiignte.pdf). They're paraphrased; the PDF (page numbers below) has the exact wording.

## Battletome (p. 52)

**Q:** Is a unit picked as the target of an Akhelian Allopex's 'Ensnared' ability ensnared for the Abyssal Tendril's 'Watery Clutch' and 'Crushing Coil'?

**A:** No.

**Q:** Is an Abyssal Tendril a separate manifestation from the Incarnate of the Deep and from other Abyssal Tendrils?

**A:** Yes, and each Abyssal Tendril adds 1 to an opponent's banishment roll (Advanced Rules, Magic 7.2: +1 for each additional enemy manifestation after the first).
