# Soulblight Gravelords FAQ

Official rulings that concern Soulblight Gravelords, from the [Rules Updates, October 2026](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_28-10_aos_core_rules_and_key_downloads_rules_updates-aghvsvstmk-u8wyh173fe.pdf). The battletome section has no FAQs; this comes from another section. It's paraphrased; the PDF (page number below) has the exact wording.

## Other rulings

**Q:** If an ability lets me re-roll one dice of an XD6 roll (e.g. the Blades of the Hollow King's 'Aurelias'), can another ability then re-roll the whole roll? (Core Rules 2.2, p. 4)

**A:** No. A dice can't be re-rolled more than once.
