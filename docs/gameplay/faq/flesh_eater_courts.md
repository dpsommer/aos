# Flesh-eater Courts FAQ

Official rulings that concern Flesh-eater Courts, from the [Rules Updates, September 2026](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_23-09_aos_core&key_rule_updates-eu6fu82jl3-dlyuiignte.pdf). They're paraphrased; the PDF (page numbers below) has the exact wording.

## Battletome (p. 44)

**Q:** When the target of 'Glimpse of Delusion' makes combat attacks because of that spell, can 'All-out Attack' or 'All-out Defence' be used in reaction to those attacks?

**A:** No.

**Q:** If a Maggotkin of Nurgle unit is the target of Ushoran's 'Glimpse of Delusion' and a model in it is slain, does that trigger its 'Vectors of Contagion'?

**A:** No.

**Q:** Does the Charnel Watch Spearhead's 'Delusions and Madness' let me pick the Delusion regiment ability I didn't pick at the start of the battle?

**A:** No. You pick from the Delusion regiment ability you picked at the start of the battle, 'Delusion of the Great Feast' or 'Delusion of the Knightly Host'.

## Other rulings

**Q:** How does an ability that refers to an enemy's Control characteristic, such as Ushoran's 'Shroudcage Fragment', work against a target with none (e.g. a manifestation)? (Core Rules 4.0, p. 4)

**A:** The target is treated as having a Control characteristic of 0.

**Q:** If an ability lets another friendly unit fight immediately after a friendly unit has fought (e.g. the Crypt Haunter Courtier's 'Knightly Exemplar'), must the second unit meet the declare requirements of the Fight ability it uses? (Core Rules 5.2, p. 4)

**A:** Yes. For example, to use 'Fight' it must be in combat or have charged that turn.
