# Ossiarch Bonereapers FAQ

Official rulings that concern Ossiarch Bonereapers, from the [Rules Updates, September 2026](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_23-09_aos_core&key_rule_updates-eu6fu82jl3-dlyuiignte.pdf). They're paraphrased; the PDF (page numbers below) has the exact wording.

## Battletome (p. 64)

**Q:** Can the target of Vokmortian's 'Voice of Nagash' be affected by passive non-Core abilities?

**A:** Yes. It's still affected by passive non-Core abilities, and it can be picked as the target of, and affected by, non-Core abilities used by manifestations, terrain, the unit itself or a player. Otherwise, for the rest of the turn it can't be picked as the target of, or affected by, non-Core abilities, and persisting effects from non-Core abilities already on it temporarily stop having an effect.

**Q:** Can a Mortek Triaxes unit with no Balefire Braziers next to it use 'Cursed Flames'?

**A:** No.

**Q:** What happens to a friendly Balefire Brazier next to an enemy unit when that enemy unit is destroyed?

**A:** It's removed from the battlefield and not used again in the battle.

**Q:** If a Mortis Reapers unit rolls a 1 on the D3 for mortal damage in 'Necrocaches', can it still move 6"?

**A:** Yes. The damage and the move are separate parts of the effect.

**Q:** Can Mortis Reapers use 'Necrocaches' when not in combat?

**A:** Yes.

**Q:** Can enemy manifestations and faction terrain features be set up within range of 'Deathly Aura'?

**A:** No. Manifestations and faction terrain are affected by enemy abilities as if they were units.

## Other rulings

**Q:** In the 'Banner of Old Ossia' artefact of power, does 'the Health characteristic of that unit' mean the unit using 'Rally' or the unit having slain models returned to it? (Other Digital Downloads, Scourge of Aqshy, p. 34)

**A:** The unit having slain models returned to it.
