# Gloomspite Gitz FAQ

Official rulings that concern Gloomspite Gitz, from the [Rules Updates, September 2026](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_23-09_aos_core&key_rule_updates-eu6fu82jl3-dlyuiignte.pdf). They're paraphrased; the PDF (page numbers below) has the exact wording.

## Battletome (p. 47)

**Q:** When the Lunar Squigs effect of 'Under the Light of the Bad Moon', or 'The Moon and the Loon', lets a Squig unit use a value of 4 for its Move characteristic, does the 4 replace the whole characteristic or just the dice roll?

**A:** Just the dice roll. Squig Hoppers (Move D6+8") would have a Move characteristic of 12".

**Q:** Can the Doom Diver Catapult's attacks be resolved one at a time? How does its Rend reduction apply then?

**A:** Yes, attacks are resolved one at a time unless you use fast dice rolling (Core Rules 17.0). The FAQ's example: attack 1 hits and wounds, and the save uses Rend 3; attack 2 fails its hit roll, is re-rolled to hit and wounds, and the save uses the now-reduced Rend 2; attack 3 fails to hit even after a re-roll, and the Rend is now 1; attack 4 hits and wounds, and the save uses Rend 1.

## Other rulings

**Q:** If a player picks the Wizard casting 'The Hand of Gork' as the spell's target, what happens? (Core Rules 28.0, p. 7)

**A:** The spell has no effect. The target can't be set up again wholly within 24" of itself, and an effect that can't be fully resolved doesn't apply at all.
